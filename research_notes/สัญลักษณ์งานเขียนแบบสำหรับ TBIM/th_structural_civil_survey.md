## 1. Structural drawings (โครงสร้าง): member tags, rebar, concrete, steel, welding, piles, schedules, general notes, and standards references

### Takeaway
Thai structural notation is a mix of (a) **formally standardized material designations**: DB/SD grades (มอก. 24-2559), RB/SR24 (มอก. 20-2559), welded wire mesh (มอก. 737-2549), hot-rolled sections (มอก. 1227), cold-formed sections (มอก. 1228-2561) and structural tubes (มอก. 107-2561); and (b) **unstandardized but very uniform office practice** for member tags (F, C, GB, B, S, ST, P …), for rebar callouts ("n-DBxx", "RBxx@s", "ป-RB6@0.15") and for schedule tables. No Thai standard was found that prescribes member-tag letters or the rebar-callout grammar. Design-code references that appear in general notes are the วสท. (EIT) standards (for example ว.ส.ท. 1008-38 and the newer วสท. 011008-21 strength-design RC standard) and the มยผ. 1101–1106-64 material and workmanship standards of the Department of Public Works and Town & Country Planning (DPT).

### Cited Findings

**Rebar: deformed bars (DB, เหล็กข้ออ้อย), มอก. 24**
- The current deformed-bar standard is มอก. 24-2559, published under ประกาศกระทรวงอุตสาหกรรม ฉบับที่ 4803 (พ.ศ. 2559) — [TISI full text TIS-24-2559](https://www.tisi.go.th/data/standard/fulltext/TIS-24-2559p.pdf)
- มอก. 24-2559 defines the grades (ชั้นคุณภาพ) SD30, SD40 and SD50. "SD40" means a minimum yield strength (กำลังจุดคราก) of 4,000 kg/cm² (ksc). The engine summary for [TISI TIS-24-2559](https://www.tisi.go.th/data/standard/fulltext/TIS-24-2559p.pdf) and [bks-steel มอก.20/24-2559 page](http://www.bks-steel.co.th/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=23&Itemid=18) report this.
- Common DB sizes are DB10, DB12, DB16, DB20 and DB25. The number is the nominal diameter in mm — [bks-steel](http://www.bks-steel.co.th/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=23&Itemid=18); [Thai Watsadu SD-40 12 mm × 12 m product](https://www.thaiwatsadu.com/th/product/%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%A5%E0%B9%87%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%82%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%AD%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%A2-(%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%87)-%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%81-%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B8%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%99-SD-40-%E0%B8%82%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%94-12-%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%A1-x-12-%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%A3-60330972)
- The market also sells an "SD40T" variant ("พับ", i.e. bent/folded bundles) alongside SD40 and SD50 — [Thai Watsadu SD-40T 16 mm](https://www.thaiwatsadu.com/th/product/%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%A5%E0%B9%87%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%82%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%AD%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%A2-(%E0%B8%9E%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%9A)-%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%81-%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B8%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%99-SD-40T-%E0%B8%82%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%94-16-%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%A1-x-10-%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%A3-(%7C-)-60194100); [Phornnarong SD40/SD40T](https://www.phornnaronglohakit.com/product/deformed-bar/). The indexed text does not say what the "T" suffix means in the standard; see Gaps.
- Example weight: SD40 DB20 at 10 m weighs 24.66 kg per bar under มอก. 24-2559 — [Sittiphan product listing](https://sittiphanmixhome.com/product/scg-deformed-steel-bar-grade-sd40-size-20-mm-length-10-m-straight/)

**Rebar callout syntax seen in Thai drawing-reading guides**
- "10-DB12" means 10 deformed bars of 12 mm, for example the column bars from footing to ground floor. "ป-RB6@0.15" means stirrups or ties (ป = ปลอก) of RB6 at 0.15 m, i.e. every 15 cm — [Bloggang "วิธีอ่านแบบก่อสร้าง สำหรับผู้ที่ไม่ใช่ช่าง" (2009)](https://www.bloggang.com/m/viewdiary.php?id=jfamily&month=08-2009&date=30&group=1&gblog=11)
- "RB9@0.20" means 9 mm round bars at 0.20 m spacing. Tags such as GB1, GB2, C1, C2, C3 locate ground beams and columns on the foundation or ground-floor plan. F1 designates a footing or pile cap — [search summary of Bloggang and "ออกแบบฐานราก F1" (Scribd)](https://www.scribd.com/document/435357827/%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%81%E0%B9%81%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%90%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%81-F1)
- Beam and column schedules (ตารางเสา / ตารางคาน) list the main-bar count and size (top and bottom), stirrup size and spacing, and remarks such as "EXTRA TOP" — [search summary, Phornnarong "เหล็กปลอก"](https://www.phornnaronglohakit.com/steel-pole-casing-beam-casing/). Low-confidence: this is the engine's summary.
- A government-published detail sheet, "รายละเอียดและข้อกำหนดเกี่ยวกับการเสริมเหล็กในพื้นทั่วไป" (standard slab reinforcement details), exists in a Thai public tender package — [Excise Dept PDF](https://www.excise.go.th/cs/groups/public/documents/document/dwnt/ndm2/~edisp/uatucm436511.pdf)
- A university structural drawing set, "ST-แบบงานโครงสร้าง.pdf", shows that the sheet-prefix "ST" convention is used for structural sheets — [CMRU ST drawing set](https://www.cmru.ac.th/attachment/get/4774/ST-%E0%B9%81%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%99%E0%B9%82%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%A3%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%87.pdf)

**Welded wire mesh (ตะแกรงเหล็กไวร์เมช)**
- Welded wire mesh is standardized by มอก. 737-2549 "ตะแกรงเหล็กกล้าเชื่อมติดเสริมคอนกรีต". It is made from cold-drawn wire welded into square or rectangular grids — [WMI Group wire mesh](https://wmigroup.co.th/wire-mesh/); [wmigroup.co.th](https://wmigroup.co.th/)

**Concrete strength notation**
- Thai design uses cylinder strength. The ready-mix trade usually quotes cube strength, so "240 ksc" can mean either one, and the two values are not equal. Drawings must state which one is meant — [SGB blog](https://www.sgb.co.th/blog/post/Strength-concrete); [ORC Premier "คอนกรีต 240 ksc"](https://orcpremier.com/article/concrete-240-ksc-strength-guide-cube-cylinder/); [HomeSmart](https://www.homesmart.co.th/content/4356/%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%B3%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%B7%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%B0%E0%B9%84%E0%B8%A3-)
- **Conflict or suspect claim:** one search summary states "240 of cylinder = 210 cube". This is physically backwards, because the cube value is always *higher* than the cylinder value for the same concrete. Do not import it. The page it came from was not identifiable among [SGB](https://www.sgb.co.th/blog/post/Strength-concrete), [PoonMix](https://poonmix.com/blogs/9d6ae/%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B5%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%9C%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%A1%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%A3%E0%B9%87%E0%B8%88-240-ksc-%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%B7%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%B0%E0%B9%84%E0%B8%A3) and [HomeSmart](https://www.homesmart.co.th/content/4356/%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%B3%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%B7%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%B0%E0%B9%84%E0%B8%A3-).
- Products are labelled with cube strength, e.g. "คอนกรีตแห้ง 240 KSC" (INSEE Mortar 52) and "CPAC Ready Mixed Concrete 240 KSC" — [Siam City Cement](https://www.siamcitycement.com/thailand/th/products/detail/insee-mortar-52); [OneStockHome CPAC 240](https://www.onestockhome.com/en/products/13626066/cpac-ready-mixed-concrete-240-ksc_cpac-scg-ready-mixed-concrete_cpac_scg)

**Concrete cover (ระยะหุ้มคอนกรีต), a typical general-notes item**
- Minimum covers commonly quoted: footings and members permanently in contact with soil 7.5 cm; beam main bars and stirrups 3 cm; columns (tied or spiral) 3.5 cm; slabs, walls and minor beams 2 cm — [Thitinan Micropile](https://www.thitinan.co.th/concrete-covering/); [Phan Group](https://www.phangroup.co.th/news/%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%B8%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%82%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B5%E0%B8%95-concrete-covering/); [saraneaknowledge](https://saraneaknowledge.blogspot.com/2020/04/covering.html?m=1). These match the values in the EIT working-stress RC standard as taught; see [tumcivil RC-WSD notes](https://engfanatic.tumcivil.com/tumcivil_1/media/RC-SR/RC-WSD.pdf). The binding table is in มยผ. 1101-64 ([ei-auditor copy](https://www.ei-auditor.com/img/16-dpt1101-64.pdf)), which could not be opened.

**Structural steel sections**
- มอก. 1227 covers hot-rolled structural sections (เหล็กโครงสร้างรูปพรรณรีดร้อน); the edition cited in 2025 articles is 2558. มอก. 1228 covers cold-formed sections (เหล็กโครงสร้างรูปพรรณขึ้นรูปเย็น). มอก. 107 covers carbon-steel tubes for general structural use — [Wazzadu มอก. steel group](https://www.wazzadu.com/article/5191); [Cotco Metalworks 2025 update](https://www.cotcometalworks.co.th/th/knowledge/371/); [H-Beam Connect TIS 1227 update](https://www.hbeamconnect.com/blog/what-is-new-in-tisi-for-hot-roll-steel/); [anyflip copy of มอก. 1227-2539](https://online.anyflip.com/mlwet/ckax/mobile/index.html)
- มอก. 1228-2561 is the current cold-formed edition. Lipped channel (เหล็กตัวซีมีขอบ, "C" or "LC" in drawings) is written H×B×lip×t in mm, e.g. 100x50x20x2.3 or 100x50x20x3.2 — [TISI TIS-1228-2561](https://service.tisi.go.th/fulltext/TIS-1228-2561m.pdf); [Global House](https://globalhouse.co.th/product/%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%A5%E0%B9%87%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%8B%E0%B8%B5%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%B5%E0%B8%82%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%9A-%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%81.-%E0%B8%82%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%94-100-x-50-x-20-x-2.3-%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%A1.-i.08014100); [bks-steel C-channel มอก.1228-2561](https://bks-steel.co.th/index.php?Itemid=68&id=20&option=com_content&view=article)
- Square and rectangular hollow tubes use grade STKR400 (JIS G3466 designation). Round structural pipe uses SSC400 / STK400 under มอก. 107-2561 — [Phornnarong STKR400/SSC400 table](https://www.phornnaronglohakit.com/stkr400-ssc400-square-round-pipe-spec/); [Phiphat Steel "วิธีดูเหล็กท่อ มอก."](https://www.phiphatsteel.co.th/content/8230/%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%98%E0%B8%B5%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%B9%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%A5%E0%B9%87%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%97%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%AD-%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%81-%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%B3%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%9A%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%A5%E0%B9%87%E0%B8%81%E0%B9%82%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%A3%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%B1%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%A7%E0%B9%84%E0%B8%9B)

**Welding**
- Thai structural steel specifications commonly require that "การใช้ไฟฟ้าเชื่อมต้องเป็นไปตาม Structural Welding Code AWS D1.1" (electric welding shall comply with AWS D1.1). This shows that American AWS practice is the usual reference in Thai notes — [OER learn.in.th document](https://oer.learn.in.th/search_detail/ZipDownload/160393)
- AWS A2.4 (current edition 2020) and ISO 2553 (2019) share the reference-line and arrow concept. They differ in how the arrow side is shown. In AWS the symbol below the line means the arrow side. ISO adds a dashed identification line to indicate the other side, and uses metric size prefixes: a = throat, z = leg, s = groove penetration — [AWS A2.4:2020 preview](https://pubs.aws.org/Download_PDFS/2__A2_4_2020_PV.pdf); [ANSI blog on A2.4:2020 changes](https://blog.ansi.org/ansi/standard-welding-symbol-change-aws-a2-4-2020/); [ISO 2553:2019 sample](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/72740/745f6baf65c1495fa159e36173dbfd21/ISO-2553-2019.pdf); [steelcalculator weld symbol chart](https://steelcalculator.app/reference/weld-symbol-chart/)
- No Thai มอก. standard for weld symbols was found.

**Piles (เสาเข็ม)**
- Pile types found in Thai practice: prestressed concrete piles (PC pile), bored cast-in-place piles (เสาเข็มเจาะ, "bored pile") and spun micropiles (สปันไมโครไพล์). Spun micropiles carry about 20–40 t per pile and are used close to existing walls. Seismic (low-strain) integrity tests are applied to both PC and bored piles, and "Side Echo" tests are used when the pile head is covered — [STS Pile Integrity Test](https://www.sts.co.th/th/blog/2020/11/pile-integrity-test); [Bhumisiam seismic test](https://www.bhumisiam.com/%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%B9%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%93%E0%B9%8C%E0%B8%82%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%87%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%B220161104/); [PPB Bored Pile seismic test](https://www.ppbboredpile.com/17266230/%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%9A-seismic-integrity-test)

**Catalog: rebar callout grammar (PRACTICE; built from the sourced examples)**
- Count and size: `{n}-{DB|RB}{d}`, e.g. `4-DB16`, `10-DB12`
- Spacing: `{DB|RB}{d}@{s}` with s in metres, e.g. `RB9@0.20`, `DB12@0.15 ม.`. Some offices write s in cm (`@15`); TBIM should parse both and normalize to metres.
- Stirrups: `ป-{RB|DB}{d}@{s}`. Variants such as "ปลอก RB9@0.15" are practice, unverified.
- Grade: `SD40` / `SR24` in general notes, not per callout (practice, unverified)
- Suggested regex: `^(?:(\d+)-)?(DB|RB)(\d{1,2})(?:@(\d+(?:\.\d+)?)\s*(ม\.|m|ซม\.|cm)?)?$`

**Concrete strength notation**
- Recommended TBIM pattern: `fc' = {value} ksc ({cylinder|cube})` or `f'c = {value} กก./ตร.ซม.`, with cylinder/cube as a required enum. The rule "Thai design = cylinder, trade = cube" is sourced. The usual rule of thumb that a 280 ksc cube is about a 240 ksc cylinder (ratio about 0.8–0.85) was not confirmed from a retrievable source.

**Sections**
- Suggested section-designation templates (practice, unverified except the C-channel format): `H {d}x{b}x{tw}x{tf}` for H-beams, `I {d}x{b}x{tw}x{tf}`, `[ {d}x{b}x{t}` for channels, `L {a}x{b}x{t}` for angles, `C {h}x{b}x{lip}x{t}` for lipped C (sourced format), `□ {a}x{a}x{t}` for square tube (STKR400) and `□ {a}x{b}x{t}` for rectangular tube.

**Welding**
- Because Thai notes reference AWS D1.1, Thai drawings most likely use AWS A2.4 symbols by default. ISO 2553 appears in projects by European or Japanese consultants. This is an inference: no Thai source compares the two.

## 2. Civil, roads and drainage (DOH / DRR / RID / DPT)

### Takeaway
Road and drainage drawing symbols in Thailand are formally set at agency level. The key document is the **DOH "คู่มือการเขียนแบบวิศวกรรมงานทาง 2558"** (road-engineering drafting manual, Bureau of Location & Design). DOH also publishes the "Standard Drawing for Highway Design and Construction" (1994 edition and 2015 revision, updated February 2564/2021). DRR publishes its own standard drawings (2556 and 2561), and RID has "มาตรฐานการเขียนแบบ กองออกแบบ 2534" plus standard drawings มฐ03/มฐ04. None of these is a TIS standard. Their symbol sheets could not be opened here, so the symbol geometry must be taken from those PDFs.

## 3. Survey, topographic and cadastral (RTSD / DOL / ALRO), datums and grids

### Takeaway
Survey conventions that are formally defined: the **RTSD L7018 1:50,000 series** (WGS84, 830 sheets, 20 m contour interval). The **DOL UTM cadastral sheet (ระวาง) system** at 1:4,000 / 1:2,000 / 1:1,000 / 1:500, with sheet names derived from the L7018 sheet number (e.g. "5136 II 9214") and set by DOL regulations (ระเบียบกรมที่ดิน 2529/2547). The DOL **หลักเขตที่ดิน** boundary monument: an RC cylinder 30 cm long and 8 cm in diameter with a coded cap. The vertical datum is **MSL at Ko Lak (เกาะหลัก), Prachuap Khiri Khan**, carried to **BM-A** at 1.4477 m. Horizontal datums are Indian 1975 (Everest 1830) on the old grid and WGS84 today, projected in UTM zones 47N and 48N.

### Cited Findings
- **RTSD L7018:** 1:50,000 topographic series with a 20 m contour interval. In พ.ศ. 2546 the 1:50,000 series changed to L7018 on the WGS84 datum, covering Thailand with 830 sheets. - RTSD map-service and geography units: [RTSD map information service centre](https://www.rtsd.mi.th/main/maps/); [RTSD geography map section](https://www.rtsd.mi.th/main/องค์กร/หน่วยงาน/กองภูมิศาสตร์/แผนกแผนที่ภูมิศาสตร์/); general map-symbol theory from [GISTDA "ชนิดแผนที่และการสร้างสัญลักษณ์"](https://gistda.or.th/news_view_new.php?n_id=2427) and [BRU "องค์ประกอบแผนที่"](https://blog.bru.ac.th/wp-content/uploads/bp-attachments/26583/%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%84%E0%B9%8C%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%9A%E0%B9%81%E0%B8%9C%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%B5%E0%B9%88.pdf)
- **Vertical datum:** Thailand's MSL (0.000 m) is the average sea level observed at Ko Lak, ต.เกาะหลัก, Prachuap Khiri Khan. It was carried to **BM-A**, the country's first benchmark, at 13°46'23.74"N / 100°31'45.39"E with an MSL height of 1.4477 m. RTSD then levelled from the Ko Lak tide-gauge benchmark through the national benchmark network — [kqgnss "พื้นหลักฐานอ้างอิงของประเทศไทย"](http://kqgnss.blogspot.com/2018/05/blog-post_15.html); [Thai Wikipedia ระดับน้ำทะเล](https://th.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%99%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B3%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%B0%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%A5); [Aboutliving](https://aboutliving.asia/sealevel_knowledge/)
- **Horizontal datums and grids:** UTM zone 47N (96°E–102°E, CM 99°E) and 48N (102°E–108°E, CM 105°E) cover Thailand (about 97°E–105°E). Indian 1975 uses the Everest 1830 ellipsoid, with a = 6,377,276.345 m and 1/f = 300.8017. It was the standard for RTSD and government maps before the change to WGS84. The RTSD (2551/2008) 3-parameter transformation is dx = 204.5, dy = 837.9, dz = 294.8 — [Civil3du zone 47/48](http://civil3du.blogspot.com/2012/12/zone-47-zone-48.html); [geomatics-tech Indian Thailand-1975](http://geomatics-tech.blogspot.com/2011/12/indian-thailand-1975-utm.html); [fastsurvey datum transformation & EGM96/2008](http://fastsurvey.blogspot.com/2014/07/wgs-84-indian-1975-geoid-egm96-egm2008.html); [ALRO UTM PDF](https://alro.go.th/uploads/org/mapping_land/download/article/article_20160807172650.pdf)
- EPSG codes: Indian 1975 / UTM zone 47N = **EPSG:24047**; zone 48N = **EPSG:24048** — [epsg.io 24047](https://epsg.io/24047); [epsg.io 24048](https://epsg.io/24048). (WGS 84 / UTM 47N = EPSG:32647 and 48N = EPSG:32648 are standard EPSG codes, but no source was fetched for them here.)
- **DOL cadastral sheets (ระวางแผนที่):** DOL produces UTM cadastral sheets at 1:4,000, 1:2,000, 1:1,000 and 1:500. Example name for a 1:4,000 sheet: **"5136II9214"**, where "5136 II" is the RTSD 1:50,000 sheet, "92" is the tens and units digits of the grid E (X) km and "14" those of N (Y). The sheet frame is 50 cm square — [DOL ระเบียบ "การสร้างระวางแผนที่" PDF](https://www.dol.go.th/media/813280638332440576/migration/2025/10/J1upVfT06cWxjawb8kRmKUED.pdf); [DOL ระเบียบกรมที่ดิน 2529 ว่าด้วยการใช้ระวางแผนที่ระบบพิกัดฉาก UTM](https://www.dol.go.th/landdoc/DocLib14/2529_3.pdf); [DOL survey doc 30-3001](https://www.dol.go.th/survey/DocLib1/30/30-3001.PDF); [DOL คำอธิบายระเบียบ…การสร้างและการใช้ระวางแผนที่ พ.ศ. 2547](https://www.dol.go.th/map/DocLib5/%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%B3%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%98%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%A3%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%87%E0%B9%81%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A3%E0%B9%83%E0%B8%8A%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%87%E0%B9%81%E0%B8%9C%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%B5%E0%B9%88%20%E0%B8%9E.%E0%B8%A8.%20%E0%B9%92%E0%B9%95%E0%B9%94%E0%B9%97.pdf)
- A DOL regulation governs field surveys for parcel detail: "ระเบียบกรมที่ดิน ว่าด้วยการรังวัดและทำแผนที่เพื่อเก็บรายละเอียดแปลงที่ดิน" — [DOL RTK network law4.pdf](https://dol-rtknetwork.com/uploads/files/law/law4.pdf). A UTM land-marker document, "เครื่องหมายที่ดินระบบพิกัดฉาก ยู ทีเอ็ม / ศูนย์กำเนิด", also exists — [kusuman.go.th PDF](https://www.kusuman.go.th/index/add_file/51wQwg9Mon32813.pdf)
- ALRO has its own standard: "มาตรฐานระวางแผนที่และแผนที่รูปแปลงที่ดินในที่ดินของรัฐ" (sheet and parcel-map standard for state land) — [ALRO PDF](https://alro.go.th/uploads/org/mapping_land/article_attach/article_attach_201806251529898717.pdf)
- **หลักเขตที่ดิน (DOL boundary monument):** a reinforced-concrete cylinder, **30 cm long, 8 cm in diameter**. Its head sits flush with the ground or structure surface. ### Inferences
- TBIM should store a project-level CRS with at least these options: `EPSG:24047`, `EPSG:24048` (Indian 1975 / UTM, legacy DOL and older drawings), and WGS84 / UTM 47N or 48N. It should also store a vertical datum of "MSL (Ko Lak)" and a sheet reference field in DOL syntax, `{L7018 sheet}{roman quadrant}{EE}{NN}[-{sub}]`, e.g. `5136II9214`. The zone split at 102°E means eastern provinces (e.g. Ubon, parts of the Northeast and East) fall in zone 48.
- A text template for the boundary monument (reproducible from the sourced description): circle; Thai series letters above the centre point; a 4-digit number below the centre, inside the circle; the arc text "หลักเขตที่ดิน" on top; the province name as arc text at the bottom. In plan it is usually drawn as a small circle with the number beside it (practice, unverified).
- Benchmark notation in drawings is usually "BM.{n} RL. +x.xxx ม.(รทก.)" (รทก. = ระดับน้ำทะเลปานกลาง). This is common Thai practice but unverified here. The abbreviation รทก. was not found in a retrieved source.

### Gaps
- The RTSD L7018 map legend (symbols for triangulation points, BMs, spot heights, contour colours, buildings, roads, vegetation, trees) was **not retrievable**. rtsd.mi.th was egress-blocked, and the searches returned no indexed legend. The published marginal legend of an L7018 sheet (or the RTSD symbol manual) is needed.
- Could not open the full DOL regulations, so these are missing: the full sheet-number syntax for 1:1,000 and 1:500 sub-sheets (e.g. "-06" subdivisions), the Thai-letter series codes on monuments (e.g. which letters are used for which document type), and the DOL parcel-map symbols.
- Existing vs proposed conventions (dashed/grey existing, solid/bold proposed) and tree symbols have no Thai source; they appear to follow generic practice.

## 4. Which items are formally standardized vs common practice

### Takeaway
Only **material designations** and **agency survey and cadastral systems** carry formal standard numbers. **Drafting grammar** (member tags, rebar callouts, schedule layout, section marks, existing/proposed styling) is not governed by any Thai national standard found. Road and irrigation drafting is governed by **agency manuals** (DOH 2558, RID 2534), which bind work for those agencies.

### Inferences
- TBIM should mark catalog rows with a `status` enum {STD, AGENCY, PRACTICE} and a `source_url`. For PRACTICE rows, allow per-office overrides, because the letter tags vary (e.g. some offices use "FC" for footing columns, or "RB" for roof beams).

- No Thai national drafting standard (a มอก. equivalent of ISO 128 / ISO 3766 for simplified rebar representation) was found. Whether TISI has adopted ISO 3766 or ISO 4066 (bar scheduling) as a มอก. is unknown and should be checked on TISI's catalogue (tisi.go.th was blocked).
